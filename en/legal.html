Privacy Policy

This page describes the information collected when you use the contact and account creation forms, or the contact means of the site. Do not send sensitive data to the form.

Responsible and Contact

The site is presented under the trademark THEPEEAK. For questions about your information, contact contact@thepeeak.com or WhatsApp at +223 69 65 66 10. The full legal identity of the person responsible shall be published in the legal notices.

Information collected and purpose

The contact form can collect your name, email address, phone, topic, expertise, budget indication and content of your message. This information is used to receive, understand and process your request, and then to respond. The account form collects the name, first name, e-mail address and password recorded as a cryptographic hash; temporary tokens are used to confirm the address or reset the password. The plain password is not stored. Contact information sent freely by e-mail or WhatsApp is processed as part of this exchange.

Recipients and providers

Requests sent from the site are stored in the Supabase database of THEPEEAK. Accounts use Supabase Auth; profiles, client projects, achievements and products for sale are kept in access-protected tables (RLS). Portfolio files are hosted in Supabase Storage; The customer rooms use a private space. Access to applications, projects and administrative functions is limited to authorized accounts.

The Supabase project is currently hosted in the eu-west-1 region. Confirmation and reset emails depend on the Supabase email configuration. Any transfers outside Mali must be examined in the light of Malian law No. 2013-015, as amended, and the procedures of the APDP.

Exchanges opened by e-mail links and WhatsApp are then processed by the courier service chosen by the person. Applications sent via WhatsApp are subject to the conditions and policy of WhatsApp/Meta.

Conservation

Requests and account data are kept in the Supabase project for the time necessary to process and provide the service. The publisher must define and apply a shelf-life, an erasure procedure and a backup period. The durations of the confirmation and reset links depend on the Auth parameters of the project.

Your requests for your data

You can write to contact@thepeeak.com to request access to, rectification or deletion of information about you, or to oppose processing in the cases provided for by law. Malian Law No. 2013-015 includes rights of access and rectification (Articles 12–13) and right of opposition on legitimate grounds (Article 19). You can also contact us.Mali Personal Data Protection Authority (PDPA).

Cookies & third party services

The site measures its audience with an internal follow-up in Supabase: page consulted, source of arrival, device category, language, estimated country and clicks to the contact means. A random session ID is stored in SessionStorage during the browser session to estimate separate visitors. The site does not store IP address or ad identifier in this audience database, and respects the "Do not follow" signal. Hearing events are automatically deleted after 13 months. Client space uses local browser storage to keep the Supabase session; disconnection deletes the local session.

To automatically choose the language, Hostinger estimates the country from the IP address and only transmits its country code to the page. If this detection is not available, the browser language will preferably be used as the initial language. Manual choice is retained locally and remains a priority. To display fonts and animations, the browser can load resources from Google Fonts, cdnjs (Cloudflare) and jsDeliver. The form forwards the requested fields to Supabase via a public key and RLS policies; e-mailing passes through the Resend service configured on the hosting. Hosting logs can record technical data, depending on the server configuration. The WhatsApp link only contacts WhatsApp/Meta if you choose to open it.

The operation and possible cookies or technical logs of these providers are governed by their respective policies: Google, Cloudflare and WhatsApp. Any subsequent addition of third party advertising or tracking tools will result in a revision of this page and the choices offered to visitors.
