Digital marketing, SEO and GEO in Bamako and Mali: the points to be clarified to start calmly.

What difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO improves page access and understanding for search engines. GEO aims at their understanding and possible use in responses generated by AI. Useful, accurate, accessible and attributable content serves both purposes.

Can we guarantee first place on Google?

No. The results depend in particular on competition, content, reputation and engine systems. We are committed to work, monitoring and transparency of results.

When can SEO work be evaluated?

Technical corrections can be noticed quickly, but their effects on research results require further exploration and time. The increase follows several comparable periods, taking into account seasonality.

Do you need a special file to appear in IA responses?

For the IA features of Google Search, Google does not request any particular IA file or special markup. Pages must be accessible, indexable and useful. Rules vary by engine and are to be monitored.

Is the advertising budget included?

It is distinguished from strategy, creation and management fees. The precise estimate which sets out the platforms, expenditure limits and monitoring procedures.

Do you optimize local visibility in Bamako?

Yes, depending on your activity and eligibility for the services concerned: consistency of name, contact information, offers and local pages. We do not create false addresses or false opinions.

How do I know where my clients come from?

We define useful events and campaign parameters, then cross-check requests with commercial returns. The tracking tools and consent are configured according to the chosen device.
