Budget for a website in Mali: How to compare estimates

A website quote is not only comparable on its total. The following are the deliverables, recurring costs and responsibilities to be reviewed before selecting.

Start with usage, not the number of pages

A showcase site presents your activity and facilitates contact. A shop adds a catalogue, orders and payment rules. A platform can manage accounts, rights, folders and connections with other tools. These projects do not require the same work.

Before asking for a price, describe the main action expected: a call, a request for a quote, a reservation, a purchase or access to a service. Then list the pages and functions required for this action. Other ideas can form a second step.

At THEPEEAK, prices are determined by quotation. The announced deadline for a showcase site is 48 to 72 hours, with the starting point and validations specified in the framing. Complex platforms require a specific schedule after study.

The six lines to compare in a proposal

PostQuestion
ContentsWho writes the texts, prepares the photos and integrates the data?
DesignWhich pages are made up and how many validations are planned?
FeaturesWhich forms, accounts, payments and connections are included?
ReferenceWhat technical and editorial optimizations will be delivered?
DeliveryWhat access, files and documents will be transmitted?
MonitoringWhat maintenance is planned and what developments will be charged separately?

Two estimates of the same amount may cover very different works. Redraft any too general line, such as "Full SEO" or "Included Maintenance", into concrete and verifiable operations.

Distinguish the creation of recurring costs

Domain, hosting, some licenses, e-mail tools and maintenance can create recurring costs. The advertising budget and the commissions of a payment provider are still other items. Ask who rules them, how often and what happens if you stop.

An inexpensive site to create can become difficult to maintain if you do not have access. Identify the domain owner, the person responsible for renewals and the conditions for transferring content.

What influences the calendar

A clear brief, available texts and authorized visuals facilitate start-up. Conversely, a translation to be produced, a connection to a software or validation of a merchant account can lengthen the steps. The calendar must integrate client returns and external dependencies.

For a complex platform, a prototype allows to check screens and rules before developing. Starting with a version centered on a useful process helps to decide the evolutions from real use.

Prepare a request for a comparable estimate

Send the same brief to providers: activity, public, objective, pages, functions, available content, constraints and desired date. Ask for a response separating the base, options and recurring costs.
