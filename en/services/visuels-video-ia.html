Answers before the estimate

Let’s talk about
the details.

IA visuals and videos in Bamako and Mali: points to be clarified to start calmly.

What formats can you produce?

Visuals for the web and networks, video sequences and campaign variations. Dimensions, duration and variants are fixed in the brief.

Is it possible to show my product faithfully?

Generation may alter details, inscriptions or proportions. For a product that requires exact reproduction, we define the items to keep from your photos and the validation steps.

Is IIA replacing a photo shoot?

Not in any case. Real images remain preferable to prove a result, present a team or document a product. We choose the approach according to usage.

Can I use the creations in advertising?

Commercial uses are set to estimate according to the elements and tools used. Images, brands, music and third-party voices must have the necessary rights.

How many corrections are possible?

The number of returns and intermediate validations are agreed before production. A complete change of direction may change the perimeter.

Can you add a voice and subtitles?

Yes, if these elements are provided: script, language, authorized voice, captioning and broadcast versions are set together.

Are the generated visuals customer evidence?

No. They may illustrate a concept or campaign, but should not be presented as a real achievement, a testimony or a measured result if they are not.
