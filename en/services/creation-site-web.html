Website creation in Bamako and Mali: points to be clarified to start calmly.

How much does a website cost in Mali?

The estimate depends on the type of site, the number of pages, the creation of content and the functions to be developed. A showcase site and a platform with customer accounts require different work. We separate creation, options and recurring fees to allow you to compare.

How long does creation take?

THEPEEAK announces 48 to 72 hours for a showcase site. The starting point, the content to be provided and the validations are specified in the framing. For a complex platform, the calendar is defined after reviewing features and integrations.

Can I modify my website after delivery?

Yes, if an administration space is part of the perimeter. We specify the contents that can be modified and take charge of the estimate. A static page can also be maintained by the studio.

Can we integrate Orange Money or Wave?

We are studying integration according to your country, your merchant account and available APIs or providers. The provider's costs, refunds and payment scenarios should be aligned separately.

Does creation include SEO?

The agreed technical and editorial fundamentals are prepared from the design stage. A content strategy and continuous SEO monitoring are distinct services. A first place on Google cannot be guaranteed.

Who owns the domain and access?

The estimate specifies the ownership of the deliverables, the licences, and the terms and conditions for granting access. We recommend that your company retain its domain name.

Are you working remotely?

Yes. The framing, modeling and validation can be done remotely, with written follow-up. Local needs, languages and means of payment are examined for each contract.
