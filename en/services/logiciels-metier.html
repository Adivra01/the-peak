Business software in Bamako and Mali: points to be clarified to start calmly.

Custom or existing software: how to choose?

We compare your rules, integrations and budget with the tools available. If an existing software meets the need, its configuration can be more suitable than a complete development.

Can we start with a small version?

Yes. A first version can cover a single process and a limited group of users, then evolve after field returns.

How are user rights managed?

We define authorized profiles and operations. Permissions must be applied on the server or database side, and not only by masked buttons.

Can I get my Excel files back?

An import is examined after examination of the quality, format and volume of the data. Preparation, correspondence and controls are included within an explicit scope.

Can my current tools be connected?

This depends on their APIs, access rights and conditions. Limits, costs and error scenarios are studied before confirming a connection.

Can software work without a connection?

Some uses may include offline operation and synchronization. It is a design constraint to express from the start, not an automatic option.

How does the software evolve after delivery?

Follow-up, incident correction and new functions are defined in the accompanying conditions. Access, documentation and maintenance responsibilities are specified.
