Branding and visual identity in Bamako and Mali: points to be clarified to start calmly.

What is the difference between a logo and a visual identity?

The logo is a sign. Visual identity also includes colors, typography, composition principles and applications. The charter explains how to use these elements together.

Can you upgrade an existing logo?

Yes. We look at the elements to be retained and those that are problematic. An evolution can preserve the already known landmarks of your customers.

What files will I receive?

The formats delivered are listed in the estimate: for example vector files for printing and exports for the web. Color and monochrome variants are defined according to your uses.

How many proposals and returns are included?

The number of tracks and return cycles is defined in the framing. This makes it possible to concentrate exchanges and avoid endless corrections.

Are rights and licences included?

The contract specifies the rights of use and assignment on creations. The policies, photos and other third party elements retain their license conditions.

Do you create print media?

We can prepare files of agreed media. Printing, quantities, paper and delivery conditions must be subject to a separate perimeter.

Can we order the identity before the site?

Yes. Setting brand codes first allows you to design a site and consistent media.
