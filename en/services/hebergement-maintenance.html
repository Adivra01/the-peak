Accommodation and maintenance in Bamako and Mali: points to be clarified to start calmly.

Can you take over a site created elsewhere?

Yes, after a diagnosis of his technology, access and condition. Some occasions require a refresher or migration.

What difference between accommodation and maintenance?

Accommodation provides the infrastructure that serves the site. Maintenance concerns its monitoring, updates, controls and interventions under the contract.

Are backups included?

The contract shall specify the content saved, the frequency, the retention, the place of storage and the restoration procedures. Their mere existence is not enough: recovery must be prepared.

Do you intervene at any time?

Support ranges and response times are explicitly agreed. We do not present a permanent penalty payment as included by default.

Can you guarantee that a site will never be hacked?

No. Updates, limited access, backups and surveillance reduce certain risks without removing any possibility of incident.

Can I keep my current supplier?

Yes if its environment is suitable for the site and the follow-up requested. Migration is proposed when the diagnosis justifies it.

Are the new pages included?

Page creations and functional changes are distinguished from maintenance. A volume of evolutions can be foreseen in the accompaniment.
