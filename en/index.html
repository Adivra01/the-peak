Before the first exchange

Your questions.
Answers Clear.

What services does THEPEEAK offer in Bamako?

THEPEEAK supports the creation of websites, SEO and digital marketing, branding, hosting and maintenance, creation of IA visuals and videos, as well as business software and automation.

How long can you create a showcase site?

The announced deadline is 48 to 72 hours for a showcase site. The necessary contents, validations and starting point are specified in the framing. The time frame for complex platforms is determined after study.

How to get a quote?

Choose an expertise or describe your need via the form or WhatsApp. We specify deliverables, options, recurring fees and schedule before proposing a quote.

Can we work together remotely?

Yes. Exchanges and validations can be done remotely. For a project in Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire or Niger, we guide local usages, means of payment and constraints before confirming the perimeter.

Does SEO guarantee customers?

No. SEOs can improve the visibility and understanding of your offers, but applications also depend on your market, reputation and ability to convert. We are looking for measurable progress, without ensuring ranking or sales.
